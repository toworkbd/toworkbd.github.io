Works best with PDFs made on a computer (Word, Excel, bank or government statements) where the text can be selected. Rows and columns are detected from the position of the text. Scanned PDFs have no text, so run them through OCR PDF first. Some old Bengali PDFs that use legacy fonts may show garbled text, because the text inside the file itself is encoded that way.

Free PDF to Excel Converter – Turn PDF Tables into XLSX

To Work BD's PDF to Excel converter turns the tables and text in a PDF into an editable Excel (.xlsx) spreadsheet. It reads the position of every piece of text, groups it into rows and columns, and can change numbers like 1,250.50 into real Excel numbers you can add up. Choose one sheet for the whole PDF or one sheet per page. It suits bank statements, result sheets, price lists and government reports.

Everything runs in your browser, so your documents stay on your device and no account is needed. Scanned PDFs should go through OCR PDF first.
