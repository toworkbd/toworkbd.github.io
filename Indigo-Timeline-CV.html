Free Indigo Timeline CV, Bio-Data & Resume Maker

The Indigo Timeline CV is a free, one-page A4 resume template with a clean white page and a modern indigo accent. Your name and photo lead the page, your phone and e-mail appear as neat chips, and every section hangs on a thin timeline with a dot marker so the page is easy to scan. Type your details on the left and watch the CV update live on the right.

Add a passport-size photo and your signature, then press Download PDF to get a ready-to-send CV for job applications, internships, scholarships and bio-data forms. No sign-up is needed.
