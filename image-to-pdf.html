Image to PDF – Convert Photos to PDF and Set the File Size Online

To Work BD’s Image to PDF tool lets you turn photos into a PDF online for free. Take a picture with your camera or choose images from your gallery, straighten and brighten each one, add as many pages as you need, and create a single PDF. You can also set the final file size to 100 KB, 200 KB, 250 KB, 300 KB, 500 KB or 999 KB, which helps when a website has an upload limit. It is useful for photos of certificates, NID copies, signatures and bills. Your images are never uploaded to any server, because everything runs inside your browser, so your documents stay private on your device. No registration, login or software installation is needed, and it works on both mobile and computer. Search Google for “image to PDF”, “JPG to PDF” or “photo to PDF with size limit” to find this To Work BD tool.
