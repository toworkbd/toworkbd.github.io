Palli Bidyut New Connection – Free Photo, Voter ID and Pratyayan Tools

This To Work BD page gathers free tools for a Palli Bidyut (Rural Electrification Board) new connection application in Bangladesh. Make a passport size photo by cropping and straightening your picture, bring a voter ID card or wiring report photo or PDF down to an exact size in KB, and create the pratyayan (no-objection certificate) PDF by writing it with your signature or by photographing a paper. When your files are ready, a link takes you to the official Palli Bidyut website to log in and submit the application. Your files are never uploaded to any server, because everything runs inside your browser, so your documents stay private on your device. No registration, login or software installation is needed. Search Google for “Palli Bidyut new connection”, “Palli Bidyut application photo size” or “passport size photo for Palli Bidyut” to find these tools.
