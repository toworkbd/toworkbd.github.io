Scan to PDF – Turn Photos of Documents into a Clean PDF

To Work BD’s Scan to PDF tool turns photos of paper documents into a clean PDF online for free, much like a scanner app. Take pictures with your camera or choose images from your gallery, and the tool straightens, crops and brightens each page automatically. You can fix the crop by dragging the four red corners, change the light, pick a filter such as Magic color, Black & white or Grayscale, choose A4 or Letter, and select High, Standard or Small file quality. It is useful for certificates, NID copies, application forms, bills and notes. Your photos are never uploaded to any server, because everything runs inside your browser, so your documents stay private on your device. No registration, login or software installation is needed. Search Google for “scan to PDF online”, “camera to PDF” or “document scanner free” to find this tool.
